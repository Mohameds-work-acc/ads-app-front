Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FlowDesk collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the product.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, company name, billing details, workspace content, support requests, and messages you send to us.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the dashboard.
  • To manage accounts, billing, and support.
  • To personalize the experience and maintain workspace data.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing Information

We may share information with service providers that help us run the product, such as hosting, analytics, billing, and support vendors. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Retention

We keep information as long as needed to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

5. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@flowdesk.app.
