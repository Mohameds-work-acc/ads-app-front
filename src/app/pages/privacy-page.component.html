Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Full Ads collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our payment support and advertising operations services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, company name, billing details, payment request details, ad campaign instructions, creative files, ad account identifiers, social media page identifiers, and messages sent to support.

We may also collect service information such as request status, invoice numbers, transaction references, campaign budgets, audience preferences, placement selections, and performance summaries needed to provide support.

2. Sensitive Information

You should not send passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers, private keys, government IDs, or unnecessary sensitive personal information. If payment processing is required, it should be completed through approved payment providers and not by sharing full card details with us.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide TikTok Coin-related payment support and status updates.
  • To create, manage, or support Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns as requested.
  • To process invoices, receipts, refunds, and account records.
  • To communicate with you about requests, service changes, compliance questions, and support issues.
  • To protect users, prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing Information

We share information only when needed to provide the service, including with payment processors, advertising platforms, hosting providers, analytics providers, support tools, professional advisers, or authorities when legally required. We do not sell personal information.

5. Platform Services

When our services involve TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, or Meta tools, those platforms may process your information under their own policies. Full Ads is independent and does not control the privacy practices, approval decisions, account actions, or payment systems of those platforms.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies, log files, and basic analytics to keep the site working, measure visits, prevent abuse, and improve support. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal or tax requirements. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is completely secure, and users are responsible for keeping their own accounts, passwords, devices, and platform access secure.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate business record requirements.

10. Children

Our services are intended for business users and people who are old enough to enter binding agreements. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. International Transfers

Because we provide online services, information may be processed in countries other than your own. We take reasonable steps to protect information according to this Privacy Policy.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact Full Ads at support@full-ads.com.
