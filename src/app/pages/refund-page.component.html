Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for FlowDesk subscriptions and related service fees.

1. Subscription Fees

Subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started, unless required by law or stated otherwise in writing.

2. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future billing, but it does not automatically create a refund for time already used.

3. Errors and Duplicate Charges

If you believe you were charged in error or more than once, contact us and include your billing details so we can review the issue.

4. Service Problems

If a technical issue prevents access to the service, we may offer a refund or credit depending on the circumstances and the amount of time affected.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email support@flowdesk.app with your name, billing email, order reference, and the reason for your request.

6. Review Time

We aim to review refund requests within two business days.
