Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are reviewed for Full Ads payment support, TikTok Coin-related workflows, and Facebook or Instagram advertising services.

1. General Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the service requested, the work already completed, the payment provider involved, and any rules or limits set by TikTok, Meta, Facebook, Instagram, or other third-party platforms.

2. Service Fees

Service fees for completed work are generally non-refundable. This includes completed support, campaign setup, account review, payment coordination, billing research, and administrative work performed at your request.

3. Advertising Budgets and Platform Charges

Advertising spend, TikTok Coin-related payments, payment processing fees, taxes, exchange rate differences, and platform charges may be controlled by third parties. Refunds for these amounts are available only when the third party allows the refund and the funds have not already been spent, transferred, locked, reversed, or made non-refundable.

4. Cancelled Requests

If you cancel before work begins, we may refund eligible service fees minus any payment processing fees or non-refundable third-party costs. If work has already started, we may deduct the value of completed work before issuing any eligible refund.

5. Rejected Ads or Platform Delays

We do not guarantee ad approval, campaign performance, payment processing speed, account status, coin availability, or platform delivery. Platform rejections, reviews, delays, account restrictions, or policy changes do not automatically qualify for a refund of completed service work.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact support@full-ads.com with your name, email, order or transaction reference, requested service, payment date, and the reason for the refund request.

7. Review Timeline

We aim to review refund requests within two business days. Approved refunds may take additional time to appear depending on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, or platform involved.
